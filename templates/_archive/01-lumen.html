01 · Engineered
01 — Engineered

Cells chosen for Indian heat.

We build with ALMM List-II domestic cells, so every module qualifies for DCR and for central subsidies. Low temperature coefficients keep output high through a Deccan summer.

  • N-type TOPCon, 16BB half-cut cells
  • −0.29 %/°C temperature coefficient
  • Anti-PID, salt-mist and ammonia tested
02 — Manufactured

Tested twice before it leaves.

Every module goes through electroluminescence imaging before and after lamination, then a Class A+A+A+ flash test. Each one carries a traceable serial number.

  • 100% EL inspection
  • Positive power tolerance 0 to +5 W
  • Serial-level traceability
03 — Installed

Certified crews, not subcontractors.

Our own technicians survey, design and install. Hot-dip galvanised structures are rated for 150 km/h winds, and we handle all the DISCOM paperwork.

  • Free shadow-analysis site survey
  • Net-metering & subsidy filing handled
  • Typical install: 1–2 days
04 — Supported

Thirty years of sunrises.

Track generation live from your phone. Our service team cleans, inspects and keeps your yield on target, backed by a 30-year linear power warranty.

  • Remote monitoring app
  • Annual health check
  • Pan-India service network
