A letter from our founder

When we started Nithyashree, the solar panels on Indian rooftops were almost all imported, and the people who sold them were rarely around when something went wrong. We thought India deserved better.

So we built a factory. Today we make our own high-efficiency modules with Indian cells, test every single one, and send our own trained crews to install them. When you call us, the person who answers works for the same company that made your panels.

Thousands of families and businesses now run on sunlight from our modules. Our promise to each of them is the same: fair prices, honest advice, and service for thirty years.

— Founder, Nithyashree Enterprises

Questions

Good to know.

How much roof space do I need?

About 80–100 sq. ft. of shade-free roof per kW. A typical 3 kW home system needs roughly 250–300 sq. ft.

Will it work during power cuts?

Standard on-grid systems switch off during outages for safety. Add a hybrid inverter with battery storage and your essential loads keep running.

What maintenance is needed?

A rinse of the panels every 2–3 weeks, and our annual health check. Our monitoring app alerts you if output drops.

Why choose DCR modules?

Only modules made with Indian cells (ALMM List-II) qualify for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy. Ours are DCR-certified, so your subsidy is protected.

Can I get a loan?

Yes. Partner banks offer collateral-free solar loans at concessional rates for residential rooftops. We help you apply.
