Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Call or WhatsApp us and an energy advisor will reply.

What types of rooftop solar systems are available?

Three configurations: on-grid (grid-tied), off-grid with lead-acid batteries, and hybrid systems that combine solar, battery storage and the grid.

What is solar energy?

Solar energy is the radiant light and heat emitted by the sun. Photovoltaic (PV) cells capture that sunlight and convert it into usable electricity.

Why choose solar energy?

It significantly reduces or eliminates monthly power costs, gives you energy independence, and cuts your carbon footprint — one of the most impactful steps a home or business can take.

How much roof area do I need?

As a thumb rule, about 80 sq ft of shade-free roof per kW. Typical requirements: 3 kW = 200–240 sq ft; 4 kW = 280–320 sq ft; 5 kW = 360–400 sq ft; 8 kW = 580–640 sq ft.

Is a site survey required?

Yes. We need the total shade-free rooftop area (north–south and east–west dimensions), photos of the terrace facing all four directions, the number of floors, and the sanctioned electrical load. Based on these details we prepare the proposal.

What models are available?

Solar water heating in 100, 200, 300 and 500 LPD capacities. Solar rooftop PV systems from 3 kW to 100 kWp in on-grid, off-grid and hybrid configurations.

How long does installation take?

Once confirmed, installation typically takes 1–4 weeks depending on system size and site conditions.

Do you handle finance and approvals?

Yes. We facilitate project finance through CapEx, OpEx and EMI models, and handle DISCOM, CEIG, net-metering and open-access filings on your behalf.
